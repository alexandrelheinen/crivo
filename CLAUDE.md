## Before starting work

1. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
2. Sync `main` and branch from it, following
   [.guidelines/workflow/branching.md](.guidelines/workflow/branching.md).
3. Read [docs/specification.md](docs/specification.md) and the feature spec
   under `docs/features/` that covers the task.

Do not merge pull requests. The owner approves and merges.
